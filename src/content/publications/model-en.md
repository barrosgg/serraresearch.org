## Research question and context

Present the research problem, territory or population of interest, and the decision the evidence aims to support. Define the timeframe, scope and contribution of the study.

## Data sources and methodology

Describe datasets, access conditions, selection criteria, microdata processing and the analytical approach. Explain baseline development and participation of local leaders where relevant.

### Traceability and data protection

Document transformations, dataset versions, aggregation rules and personal data safeguards. Include materials needed to reproduce the analysis without exposing people or communities.

## Findings and interpretation

Include only findings that were obtained and reviewed. Charts should provide sources, units, dates, legends and textual descriptions. Supply accessible tables containing the data behind each visualization.

> This template contains no findings, figures or claims from a completed research study.

## Limitations and uncertainty

Explain gaps, biases, assumptions and limits to inference. Distinguish association, prediction and causation; explain when findings cannot be generalized.

## Implications for decisions

Connect findings to the original question. Describe possible applications and the conditions for using the evidence responsibly.

## References and materials

List complete references, links to public sources, access dates and licenses. Add a DOI and PDF only when real files and valid identifiers exist. Declare credits, funding and conflicts of interest as appropriate.
